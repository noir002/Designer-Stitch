Our story

Not Just a Print Shop.

Designer Stitch was born from a frustration with generic clothing. If you're going to wear something every day, it should say something about you.

We work directly with artists, customers and small brands to create pieces that wouldn't exist otherwise. Custom fit, custom colours, custom artwork.

From a single statement tee to a full branded collection — you bring the idea, we bring it to life on premium fabric.
